Can another car use the bay while the first car is still driving there?

Yes, if the bay is booked from the car's arrival time and not from the moment it sets off. Press Play and watch one evening unfold. Cars appear one at a time, as they would in real life.

How this runs in the real world

  1. A car asks for a charge. The driver app or fleet telematics sends location and battery level.
  2. The system works out the ETA. The bay is booked for the arrival time only, so it stays free until then.
  3. Every new request triggers a re-plan. Cars that have not left can be given a better slot. Cars already on the road keep theirs.
  4. Drivers get one message: which bay, and when to leave. A driver is only re-routed if it saves more than ₹5.

What a real deployment still needs

  • Live ETAs. If the first car is early, or the gap car overruns, one of them queues. This demo assumes cars are on time, so a real system needs a safety margin.
  • Finer time slots. The model uses 30-minute slots, so gaps shorter than that are not used.
  • Charger data. Bay status from the operator (for example over OCPP), because bays also get used by the public.

Everything on this page uses a synthetic fleet and an illustrative tariff, with Kochi-area stations approximated. These are simulation results, not field measurements.
